Yes, CADCraft is free. It is open source under the MIT or Apache-2.0 license, with no account and no subscription.

It runs on Windows 11, macOS, Linux and FreeBSD, and in a modern web browser through a WebAssembly build. See [Install CADCraft](../getting-started/install.md).

CADCraft is in early development. It follows the AutoCAD workflow, but it does not do everything AutoCAD does yet. It is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Autodesk. You can follow progress in the [roadmap](https://github.com/storytold/cadcraft/blob/main/ROADMAP.md).

It can be installed through ArtCraft Launcher, and it is one of the Craft apps from the ArtCraft team. Ask questions in [Discord](https://discord.gg/artcraft).
